You tapped a fake link.

You scanned a QR code and opened the link without checking where it went. That is exactly what scammers hope for. You are safe this time, because this page asks for nothing.

If this had been a real scam page

It could have asked for your card number, your bank login or your OTP.

What would have protected you

  1. Point the camera. Do not tap yet.
  2. Read the link your phone shows.
  3. Check the owner. An official Singapore government site ends in .gov.sg.
  4. Safest of all: skip the QR and search for the app in your app store.

This page is part of a scam awareness workshop. It has no forms and does not ask for any information.
